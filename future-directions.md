#                                                         10 Mad God-Approved Ideas for p2prig 🎭

## 1. Cheese-Powered Mining (The Sheogorath Special)
- Replace proof-of-work with proof-of-cheese
- Miners must submit verified Gouda hashes
- The older and smellier the cheese, the higher the hash weight
- "The Wabbajack of Mining" - completely random rewards (sometimes you get cheese, sometimes you become cheese)

## 2. The Shivering Isles Expansion
- Split the network into Mania (high-risk, high-reward mining) and Dementia (unpredictable but massive payouts)
- Miners randomly shift between realms based on moon phases
- "Haskill's Revenge" mode - every 10,000th block reverses transaction history for lulz

## 3. Daedric Pool Hopping
- Mining pools are replaced by Daedric Princes
- Each Prince offers unique buffs/maluses
- Molag Bal's pool steals 10% hashrate but doubles rewards
- Sanguine's pool gets progressively drunker (and less efficient) the longer you mine

## 4. The Wabbajack Protocol
- Randomly transforms your mining algorithm mid-hash
- One in a million chance to instantly solve a block (or turn it into a sweetroll)
- "It's all fun and games until someone loses a nonce!"

## 5. Sheogorath's Madness Meter
- The longer you mine, the more "mad" you become
- At maximum madness, your miner starts submitting Shakespearean sonnets as valid blocks
- Bonus rewards for the most creatively insane solutions
- "I'm not crazy, my reality is just different than yours!"

## 6. The Fork in the Road
- Every time consensus is almost reached, the chain splits into two competing realities
- Miners must choose which reality to support
- The losing reality gets turned into cheese (see point #1)

## 7. The Skooma Rush
- Temporary 10x hashrate boost
- Side effects include: blocks appearing where there are none, talking to your GPU, and seeing the fabric of the blockchain
- "You haven't lived until you've seen the blockchain in 11 dimensions!"

## 8. The Pelagius' Parlor Puzzle
- Every 1000th block is a logic puzzle
- Solve it to unlock the "Mind of Madness" mining mode
- Fail and your miner recites bad poetry for an hour
- "The only winning move is to play both sides!"

## 9. The Gray Cowl of Nocturnal
- Anonymous mining mode
- Your hashrate becomes invisible to network stats
- 1% chance to "steal" another miner's block reward
- "They see me hashing, they hatin'..."

## 10. The Grand Finale: CHIM Mode
- Realize you're just a character in a simulation
- Unlock console commands
    - tgm - Toggle God Mode (infinite hashrate)
    - player.additem f 1000000 - Just kidding, that never works here
- "Wait... if I'm just code... who's dreaming who?"

## Bonus Round: The Sweetroll Heist
- Every block contains a hidden sweetroll
- Steal other miners' sweetrolls for bonus hashrate
- "Let me guess... someone stole your sweetroll?"
